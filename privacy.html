Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Busters is an app that helps fan costuming and charity groups ("crews") organize events, sign-ups and chat. It is run by Aerial West LLC, a California company ("Busters", "we"). Each crew decides who is added to it. This page explains what Busters keeps, who can see it, and how to have it removed. Busters is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Pictures or Columbia Pictures.

What Busters keeps

  • Your account: your name, the email you sign in with, and the crew you belong to.
  • Details you or your crew add: callsign, phone, Discord name, Instagram, birthday (month and day), city, membership dates, dues status and roles.
  • Event activity: which events you answer Going, Maybe or No to, notes you leave, and whether you sent event photos.
  • Things you post: chat messages, photos and files sent through Busters, and attachments added to events.
  • An activity log of sign-ins and changes, so admins can keep records accurate.
  • Discord link: if your crew uses Discord, your Discord user ID, so buttons you tap there count as your answers.

Busters does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Your browser stores your sign-in session so you stay signed in.

Who can see it

  • You can see and edit your own details on the Me tab.
  • Your crew's admins and Personnel can see the details your crew keeps on you, to run the crew.
  • Other members of your crew see your name, callsign, Discord name, event sign-ups and your city if you put yourself on the member map. Your phone and email are shown only if you turn them on under Contact in My details.
  • The event's point of contact can be called or texted by members working that event.
  • People outside your crew cannot see your information. Event attachments are stored at private, hard-to-guess links so they can be shown on your crew's Discord.

Services Busters uses

Busters relies on these providers to run. Each only gets what it needs for its part:

  • Supabase stores the database and files.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host the app and its web address.
  • Resend sends sign-in emails.
  • Discord, if your crew connects it: event cards, threads and chat messages are posted there under your name.
  • Google, if your crew connects it: Google Drive for event photos, Google Calendar and Sheets for your crew's event list, and Google Maps for directions.
  • Dropbox, if your crew connects it, for photo upload links.

Event photos and Google Drive

A crew can connect a Google Drive account so event photos and videos are saved there. Busters asks Google only for access to files it creates itself (the "drive.file" permission). It cannot see, open, or change anything else in that Drive. It uses that access only to make a "Busters Photo Drop" folder, one folder per event inside it, and to save the photos and videos members send. Busters passes those files straight through and does not keep copies.

Busters stores only the connection key Google provides and the account's email address, so admins can see which account is connected. To disconnect, remove Busters at myaccount.google.com/connections, or ask a crew admin to disconnect it in the app. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Dropbox

Crews can also connect Dropbox for upload links. Files sent that way are saved in the connected Dropbox and may be moved on to the crew's Google Drive, then removed from Dropbox.

What Busters never does

  • Sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • Use your information or files for anything other than running your crew.

Keeping and deleting your information

Busters keeps your information while you are part of a crew that uses it. You can change most of your details yourself on the Me tab. To have your account and details removed, ask your crew's admin or email hello@busters.app. Removed information may stay in backups for a short time before it is overwritten. Messages already posted to Discord are kept by Discord and can be deleted there.

You can also email us to ask what Busters holds about you or to correct it.

Children

Busters is not meant for children under 13, and crews should not add them.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and let crews know.
