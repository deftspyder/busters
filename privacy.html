BUSTERS

Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Busters is a volunteer-run app that helps fan costuming groups, such as the Los Angeles Ghostbusters, organize charity events. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Pictures.

What Busters keeps

  • Your name, email, and the details you add to your profile. Your phone number is private unless you choose to share it.
  • Which events you sign up for, and an activity log of sign-ins and answers so admins can keep things accurate.
  • If you link Discord, your Discord user ID, so buttons you tap there count as your answers.

Event photos and Google Drive

A group can connect a Google Drive account so event photos and videos are saved there. Busters asks Google only for access to files it creates itself (the "drive.file" permission). It cannot see, open, or change anything else in that Drive. It uses that access only to make a "Busters Photo Drop" folder, one folder per event inside it, and to save the photos and videos members send. Busters passes files straight through and does not keep copies.

Busters stores only the connection key Google provides and the account's email address, so admins can see which account is connected. To disconnect, remove Busters at myaccount.google.com/connections, or ask a group admin to disconnect it in the app. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Dropbox

Groups can also connect Dropbox for upload links. Files sent that way are saved in the connected Dropbox, and may be moved on to the group's Google Drive.

What Busters never does

  • Sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • Use your files or data for anything other than running your group's events.

Deleting your information

Ask your group's admin and your account and its details will be removed.
